EhTagTranslation/Database
https://github.com/EhTagTranslation/Database

Used in BUNKO for: the Japanese-script names attached to E-Hentai artist / group /
character / parody tags (search-vocabulary.json entries whose evidence method is
"ehtag-translation-name", 660 entries). Only the name strings are used; descriptions
and images are not copied.
